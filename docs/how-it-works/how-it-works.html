iGovPath · How it works

One platform, three parts, one rule.

iGovPath brings government jobs, internships and learnerships into one place. Departments publish once; applicants find posts, get a checklist and a plain-language explanation, and hear about changes.

AI proposes. A named official approves. Nothing reaches applicants without approval.

The parts and how they talk

Web app

The site and the officer portal. It decides who may do what and records who did it.

AI service

Does the reading and writing. Every fact the AI returns must be quoted from the source, or it is blanked.

Database

Shared, online storage. The whole team sees the same posts, users and history.

When an officer publishes an advert

1
OfficerUploads a PDF in the portal: one advert, a whole circular, an amendment notice or a form.
2
AI serviceReads the text with page numbers. A circular is split into one piece per post, each with its department's closing date and how-to-apply section.
3
GroqSuggests the fields (title, reference number, closing date, requirements, documents, how to apply) and quotes the sentence each one came from.
4
AI serviceChecks every quote really appears in the advert. Unsupported values are blanked and flagged. If the advert doesn't say it, the record says "not stated".
5
OfficerSees each field beside the highlighted source, corrects anything, and clicks Approve and publish. Officers can only approve their own department's records.
6
DatabaseStores it as a published post, version 1, with the officer's name and department in the audit log.

When an applicant uses it

  • Search and post pages read straight from the database. No AI involved.
  • Checklist is built by AI from the approved record. The current Z83, CV, certified ID and the reference number are always added by code.
  • Plain language in English or isiZulu, with the original wording beside it. Every number is checked against the record.
  • Save a post to get a reminder 3 days before it closes.
  • What can I apply for? compares qualification, licence and province with each post's stated requirements, using fixed rules. It never scores or ranks people.

AI results are saved per post version, so later visitors get them instantly.

When an amendment arrives

1
OfficerUploads the amendment notice.
2
AI serviceFinds each post it changes by reference number, and what changed.
3
OfficerSees before and after with the quoted evidence, and confirms.
4
Web appSaves version 2, marks the post Amended or Extended, rebuilds its checklist and notifies everyone who saved it.
Real example in the app: Circular 42 of 2026 changes the requirements of the Eastern Cape Social Development post DSD 02/11/2026 from Circular 39.

Who can do what

RoleHow they get inWhat they can do
AnyoneNo account neededSearch posts, read posts, checklists and explanations, ask the assistant
ApplicantSigns up with email and passwordSave posts, tick checklists, get reminders and change notifications
OfficerSigns up, requests access, an admin approvesUpload, review and approve records for their own department; dashboard, forms, audit log
AdminThe account using ADMIN_EMAILApprove or reject officers, assign departments, deactivate users, see all departments

Built-in safeguards

  • Every AI fact carries a quote from the source; code checks it.
  • Nothing is published without a named officer's approval.
  • Every change is in the audit log: who, what, when.
  • AI never scores, ranks or rejects applicants (POPIA s71).
  • Only public documents go to the AI; personal data is blocked.
  • The dashboard shows anonymous counts only, never names.
